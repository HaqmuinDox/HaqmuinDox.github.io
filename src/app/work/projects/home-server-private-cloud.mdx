I run a home server so my files, photos and passwords live on hardware I own instead of in
someone else's cloud. It is a small Fujitsu ESPRIMO (a quad-core i5 with 16 GB of RAM) running
Ubuntu, and over time it has grown into a dozen-plus containers.

What runs on it:

- **Nextcloud** for files, contacts and calendars
- **Immich** for photos, with the machine's integrated GPU handling the transcoding
- **Vaultwarden** as a password manager
- **Dawarich** for private location history
- **Navidrome** for music, plus a small local LLM stack (Ollama and Open WebUI)
- **Pi-hole** with Unbound as the DNS resolver for the whole network
- **n8n** for small automations

Most of it is Docker Compose, with Watchtower keeping the images current. The part I find most
interesting is the networking. Nothing is exposed to the open internet: every service is reachable
only from devices on my Tailscale tailnet, and Caddy terminates HTTPS internally with certificates
I renew over DNS. A nightly job mirrors the important data to a separate disk. I wrote the whole
setup up, container by container, in [the blog](/blog/docker-homelab).
